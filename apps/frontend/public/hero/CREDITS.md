# Sport photography

Two groups, and the difference matters:

**Genuine Rwandan photography** (Wikimedia Commons, credited below): football,
basketball, volleyball, athletics, cycling, amashuri. These rotate in the hero and
the hero prints their attribution, which CC BY-SA requires.

**Curated stock, pulled local** (Unsplash): handball, netball, swimming, tennis,
judo, boxing, chess. No Rwandan photograph of these sports exists on Commons — a
search returned Ryanair aircraft for "Rwanda tennis" and a Delhi Commonwealth
Games bout for "Rwanda boxing". These are the same images sportThemes.ts already
used, downloaded so the page needs no network. They are the right SPORT, checked
by eye. Replace each with real MINISPORTS photography at the same filename.

Two Rwandan candidates WERE found and rejected on sight: a grayscale documentary
photograph of a child bathing in a river (wrong register for a federation's
swimming tile) and a blurry hotel-lobby phone snap of a carved chess set.


**Every file here was opened and visually checked before use.** A seventh image,
`football-crowd.jpg`, was downloaded and DELETED: Commons filed it under Amahoro
Stadium, Kigali, but the supporters in it are Congolese (DRC flags, "CONGO"
sashes). A wrong photograph is worse than no photograph.

Rwandan sport photography from Wikimedia Commons. Every file below is freely
licensed; the CC BY / CC BY-SA ones REQUIRE the attribution kept with them.
Replace any of these with MINISPORTS press photography at the same filename and
nothing else needs to change.
