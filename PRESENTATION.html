One digital home for Rwandan sport

PESTEL · 1 of 3

Political & Economic

Political TAILWIND

  • Sport & ICT are national priorities (MINISPORTS, Smart Rwanda, e-gov via Irembo)
  • Sport as nation-branding: Visit Rwanda kits, hosting the BAL and the 2025 UCI Road Worlds in Kigali
  • Government-backed school sport (Kagame Cup / Amashuri) aligns directly with the AKC3 module
  • Value depends on federation & government adoption

Economic MIXED

  • Widespread mobile money (MoMo, Airtel) enables the subscription model
  • Growing sport economy & sponsorship (BK, MTN, Skol); digital job creation
  • Price sensitivity on verification fees; low ARPU
  • FX exposure — USD-priced cloud vs RWF revenue
PESTEL · 2 of 3

Social & Technological

Social STRONG FIT

  • Very young population (median ≈ 20) + high mobile penetration
  • Sport as a vehicle for unity & social cohesion
  • Trilingual UI (Kinyarwanda / French / English) built in
  • Gender & disability inclusion modeled explicitly
  • Urban–rural digital-literacy & connectivity divide

Technological ENABLING

  • National ICT push — 4G/fibre, Kigali Innovation City
  • Mobile-first PWA with an offline shell fits the market
  • Live data is never cached → fresh scores on weak networks
  • Headroom for analytics / talent-scouting later
PESTEL · 3 of 3

Environmental & Legal

Environmental ALIGNED

  • Rwanda is a green leader — plastic-bag ban, Car-Free Days, cycling culture (Tour du Rwanda)
  • Digital replaces paper/spreadsheet processes → paperless governance
  • Low carbon footprint of a cloud service
  • Rainy-season scheduling for outdoor sport

Legal WATCH

  • Data Protection Law Nº 058/2021 (NCSA) — personal data, national IDs, and minors' documents (AKC3)
  • Payments under BNR + gateway compliance
  • Postgres portability supports data-residency needs
  • Encodes federation statutes & eligibility/transfer rules
