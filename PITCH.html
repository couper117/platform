RwaSportRwanda National Sports Platform · RNSP

The digital home of Rwandan sport — every fixture, result and athlete, from the pro league to the school playground.

1 The problem

Rwandan sport — federations, leagues, clubs and thousands of school athletes — still runs on paper and spreadsheets. Results are slow, records are lost, verification is manual, and fans have no single place to follow it all. There is no digital backbone for a sector the country is investing in heavily.

2 The solution

One mobile-first platform that digitizes the whole sports lifecycle: federation & team governance, player verification, fixtures, live scores, standings and discipline — plus a dedicated inter-school module (Amashuri / AKC3) and racing. Built for Rwanda: trilingual, offline-ready, inclusive by design.
